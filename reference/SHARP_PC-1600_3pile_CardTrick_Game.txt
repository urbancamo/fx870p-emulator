GAME INSTRUCTIONS:

How to Play the 3-Pile Card Trick Game:

This is a SHARP PC-1600 computer version of a classic three‑pile card trick. 
You (the user) think of a card, and after three rounds of dealing and asking 
which pile it’s in, the computer reveals your card.

**Setup**  
- The deck contains 36 cards: 4 suits (hearts, diamonds, clubs, spades) each 
with ranks 6, 7, 8, 9, 10, Jack (J), Queen (D), King (K), Ace (A).  
- The computer shuffles them randomly at the start.  

**Gameplay**  
1. You are asked to **remember one card** from the deck.  
2. The computer deals the cards into **three columns** (piles) on the screen.  
   - It takes three cards at a time and places one in each column from left to right.  
   - For each card, it displays a graphical suit symbol and the 
rank (e.g., a heart symbol with "7" below it).  
   - After each set of three cards, the program waits for you to press the **SPACE bar** to continue.  
3. When all 36 cards have been shown (12 groups of three), the screen clears and asks:  
   **"Column (1-3):"**  
   You must type the number of the column (1, 2, or 3) that contained your chosen card.  
4. The computer then rearranges the deck so that the column you chose becomes the **middle** pile, and then repeats the entire dealing process two more times (three rounds total).  
5. After the third round, the computer displays a single card – the one at position 18 in the deck – and announces:  
   **"This is the card you thought of!"**  

**Why it works**  
Each time you identify the pile, your card moves toward the centre of the deck. After three such moves, it ends up exactly at the 18th position, which the computer then reveals.



Try It Yourself and support Handheld BASIC Computing From Japan

1. Run the program.  
2. When the first screen appears, think of any card (e.g., "7 of Clubs").  
3. Press any key to start the dealing.  
4. Watch the cards appear in three columns. When you see your card, remember which column it’s in (left = 1, middle = 2, right = 3).  
5. After all cards are shown, type that column number.  
6. The process repeats two more times. Each time, note the column where your card appears.  
7. After the third input, the computer will display your card – and it will be the one you thought of!
