Summary of Behavior

  • Default: side-by-side (⬒) — DOC panel to the right of BASIC panel
  • Toggle: click ⬒/⬓ icon in DOC header to switch to stacked (full width below)
  • Preference saved to localStorage — remembered across sessions
  • Auto-stack on narrow screens — forced to stacked below ~600px width regardless of preference
  • Standalone DOC (no BASIC panel open) — always full width, no toggle needed
